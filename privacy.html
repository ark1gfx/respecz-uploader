Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This policy explains what ReSpecz Uploader ("the tool") does with data when you connect your TikTok account.

1. Data we access

  • Basic profile (user.info.basic, user.info.profile): your TikTok display name, avatar, username and open ID, to show which account is connected.
  • Account statistics (user.info.stats): your follower, following, like and video counts.
  • Your public videos (video.list): titles, cover images, links and the view, like, comment and share counts of your own recent videos.
  • Uploading (video.upload): permission to send a video you choose to your TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is posted until you post it yourself in TikTok.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok after you log in, so the tool can upload on your behalf.
  • Videos and captions you choose to upload.

2. How it is used

The data is used only to (a) send your videos to your own TikTok drafts when you ask the tool to, and (b) show you your own account and video statistics in a private dashboard that runs on your computer. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is not combined with data about other people.

3. Storage

Access tokens and settings are stored locally on the computer running the tool. Videos are read from that computer and sent directly to TikTok. No data is stored on any ReSpecz server.

4. Sharing

Data is only sent to TikTok through its official API. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.

5. Retention and deletion

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services). Deleting the tool's local settings file removes the stored tokens.

6. Children

The tool is not intended for users under 13, or the minimum age required by TikTok in your country.
